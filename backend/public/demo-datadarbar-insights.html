This tenant is configured with useKbOnly: true — it has no baked-in dataset in its system prompt. Answers depend entirely on documents uploaded via the admin panel's Knowledge Base page (Qdrant-backed retrieval). Until real survey documents are uploaded and indexed, expect the bot to say it doesn't have that information yet, rather than guessing.
